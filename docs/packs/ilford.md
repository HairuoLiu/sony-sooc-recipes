## Honest note

These are **approximations**, not Ilford's actual film tonal science. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings (Creative Style, contrast, sharpness, exposure compensation, DRO) — it cannot load real film emulsions. Treat them as tasteful starting points, then tweak to your taste.
