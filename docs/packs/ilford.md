**At a glance:** this pack compiles **9** recipes — **5** black-and-white Ilford film-look recipes from the **Ilford** group, plus **4** motion-picture looks (Cinestill + Rec.709) from the **Cine** group. The Cine group is a motion-picture look family, **not** an Ilford product; it is grouped into this pack for distribution convenience.

## Honest note

The Ilford entries are **approximations**, not Ilford's actual film tonal science; the Cinestill / Cine entries are motion-picture looks, not a film emulsion. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings (Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, white balance, exposure compensation, DRO) — it cannot load real film emulsions. Treat them as tasteful starting points, then tweak to your taste.
