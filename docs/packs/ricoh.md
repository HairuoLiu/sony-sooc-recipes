## Honest note

These are **approximations**, not Ricoh's own colour science. A Sony body in the a6000 class stores only ordinary settings (Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, an optional Picture Effect, exposure compensation and D-Range Optimizer) — there is **no tone curve, LUT, or Log** behind any of these. Where a Sony Picture Effect is used it overrides the Creative Style entirely. The last three entries (Cinema Green/Yellow and Moriyama) are community-authored and not lab-verified; treat them as starting points to tweak to taste.
