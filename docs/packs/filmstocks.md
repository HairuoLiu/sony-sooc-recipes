## Honest note

These are **approximations**, not Fujifilm's own film chemistry. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings (Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, an optional Picture Effect, exposure compensation, DRO) — it cannot load real film emulsions. A few entries are community-authored and not lab-verified; treat them as starting points to tweak to taste.
