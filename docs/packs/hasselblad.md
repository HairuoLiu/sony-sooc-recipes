## Honest note

These are **approximations**, not Hasselblad's actual HNCS colour science. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings (Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, DRO) — it cannot reproduce a medium-format colour engine. Treat them as tasteful starting points, then tweak to your taste.
