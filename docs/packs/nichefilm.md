**At a glance:** this pack compiles **26** recipes — **5** black-and-white Ilford film-look
recipes from the **Ilford** group, **4** motion-picture looks (Cinestill + Rec.709) from the
**Cine** group, and **17** heritage / niche film-stock looks (Agfa, Polaroid, Ferrania and
others) from the **Other Stocks** group. The Cine group is a motion-picture look family, **not**
an Ilford product, and Other Stocks is a mixed bag of heritage brands with no single brand behind it.

## Honest note

The Ilford entries are **approximations**, not Ilford's actual film tonal science; the Cinestill / Cine
entries are motion-picture looks, not a film emulsion; and the Other Stocks entries are a **mixed bag of
heritage brands** (Agfa, Polaroid, Ferrania, Adox, ORWO, Rollei, Lomography, Svema, Konica, Astrum,
Prokudin-Gorskiy and others) with no single brand behind them — `other-stocks` previously shipped only
inside the all-in-one app. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings
(Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, white balance, exposure compensation, DRO) — it cannot
load real film emulsions. Treat them as tasteful starting points, then tweak to your taste.
