## Honest note

These are **approximations**, not any brand's actual colour science. An a6000-class Sony body can only store ordinary in-camera settings (Creative Style, saturation, contrast, sharpness, picture effects, exposure compensation, DRO) — it cannot load real film emulsions or another maker's colour engine. Treat them as tasteful starting points, then tweak to your taste.
